Sources & References: Novartis

This document identifies the sources behind each factual claim in the accompanying Novartis Retirement Planning flyer. Produced for educational and compliance transparency purposes.
All factual claims in the flyer are grounded in publicly available government guidance, employer-disclosed public information, or clearly labeled as illustrative examples. Figures marked "illustrative" should be verified with Novartis's official Benefits Portal or HR department.
Claim in Flyer Source
IRS / Tax Rules
2025 401(k) employee contribution limit: $23,500
IRS — Retirement Topics: 401(k) Contribution Limits Published annually by the IRS. Find at IRS.gov under "Retirement Plans" → "Plan Participant/Employee" → "Retirement Topics – 401(k) and Profit-Sharing Plan Contribution Limits"
Age 50+ catch-up contribution limit: $7,500 (2025)
IRS — Retirement Topics: Catch-Up Contributions Same IRS source as above. The catch-up amount is indexed and announced each October for the following year. Verify annually at IRS.gov.
Pre-tax 401(k): contributions reduce current taxable income; withdrawals taxed in retirement
IRS Publication 590-A: Contributions to Individual Retirement Arrangements IRS.gov → Publications → Publication 590-A. Also see IRS.gov/retirement-plans for an overview of traditional vs. Roth treatment.
Roth 401(k): no upfront tax deduction; qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free
IRS — Designated Roth Accounts in 401(k) Plans IRS.gov → "Retirement Plans" → "Designated Roth Accounts." See also IRS Publication 590-B for distribution rules.
ESPP — IRS Section 423 plans; tax treatment of discounted shares
IRS Publication 525: Taxable and Nontaxable Income IRS.gov → Publications → Publication 525. Covers tax treatment of employee stock purchase plans under Section 423. Also see IRS Form 3922 guidance.
Employer Benefits — Novartis
Novartis offers a 401(k) Savings Plan, Cash Balance Pension, and Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
Novartis US Careers / Benefits Overview; Novartis Annual Reports Novartis publicly discloses benefits in career materials at novartis.com/careers and in annual proxy statements filed with the SEC (SEC EDGAR, Form DEF 14A). Verify specifics through the Novartis Benefits Portal.
Employer match structure (labeled "illustrative" in the flyer)
Illustrative example — not sourced to a specific figure The match rate shown in the flyer is an illustrative example based on industry benchmarks (BLS National Compensation Survey). Actual Novartis match should be confirmed in the official plan documents or Summary Plan Description (SPD).
Cash Balance Pension: annual pay credits + interest credits; lump sum or annuity option
U.S. Department of Labor — What You Should Know About Your Retirement Plan DOL.gov → Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA) → Publications. Describes how cash balance plans work generally. Novartis-specific credits are in the Summary Plan Description, available from HR.
Vesting schedule (3–5 year graded, labeled "illustrative")
ERISA § 203; U.S. Department of Labor — Types of Retirement Plans ERISA sets minimum vesting standards. Actual Novartis vesting schedule is in the plan's SPD. DOL.gov → EBSA → "Types of Retirement Plans."
Social Security
Social Security claiming ages: early at 62, full retirement age 67 (born 1960+), maximum at 70
Social Security Administration — When to Start Receiving Retirement Benefits SSA.gov → Benefits → Retirement → "When to Start Receiving Retirement Benefits." Full retirement age varies by birth year; see SSA retirement age chart.
25–30 year retirement income horizon planning guideline
Social Security Administration Life Expectancy Calculator; Society of Actuaries RP-2014 Mortality Tables SSA.gov/oact/population/longevity.html for life expectancy data. Society of Actuaries publishes mortality tables used by pension actuaries. This is a general planning guideline, not a guarantee.
Healthcare / Medicare
Medicare eligibility begins at age 65
Medicare.gov — When Does Medicare Coverage Start? Medicare.gov → "Get Started with Medicare" → "When does Medicare coverage start?" Published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).
Planning Concepts
Diversification: avoid over-concentration in a single investment, including employer stock
SEC.gov Investor Education — Asset Allocation, Diversification, and Rebalancing SEC.gov → Investor.gov → "Introduction to Investing" → "Investment Strategies and Types." Also: FINRA.org → "Asset Allocation" and DOL's "Taking the Mystery Out of Retirement Planning."
As retirement approaches, shift toward lower-volatility holdings (bonds, stable-value)
DOL — Taking the Mystery Out of Retirement Planning U.S. Department of Labor, Employee Benefits Security Administration. Available at DOL.gov/general/topic/retirement. Also see FINRA's guidance on target-date funds and glide paths.
Pension + 401(k) + Social Security = complementary income streams
DOL — Savings Fitness: A Guide to Your Money and Your Financial Future DOL.gov / EBSA publications. Also referenced in SSA's "Retirement Benefits" publication and CFPB's "Planning for Retirement" resource guide.
